# FLO — Glamping Premium Florianópolis
## Prompts para o ChatGPT gerar IMAGENS + MASTER PLAN

Fonte: "FLO — Ativo Imobiliário Premium Glamping" (27 páginas).
Uso: cole primeiro o **Bloco 0 (contexto + estilo)** numa conversa nova do ChatGPT
com geração de imagem ativada. Depois cole **um prompt por vez** (Bloco 1 = imagens,
Bloco 2 = master plan). Os prompts estão em inglês porque o modelo de imagem
responde melhor assim; os títulos e instruções estão em português.

---

## BLOCO 0 — CONTEXTO E ESTILO (colar uma única vez, no início)

```
You are an architectural visualization artist and landscape masterplanner
working for a premium hospitality developer. Everything you generate in this
conversation refers to ONE real project. Memorize this brief:

PROJECT: "FLO" — a premium luxury glamping resort, already in operation, in
Florianópolis, Santa Catarina, Brazil (north of the island, Vargem Pequena /
Ratones valley, access by the Via Panorâmica VP-513).

SITE: 28,603 m² (about 3 hectares), continuous and regular plot, gentle
favourable topography, inside a preserved Atlantic Rainforest (Mata Atlântica)
valley, surrounded by green forested hills, 10–15 min from the north beaches
(Jurerê Internacional). Existing elements: a small natural pond, a stone-and-
timber perimeter wall, mature trees (fig trees, palms, flamboyants), meadows,
a wooden footbridge and forest trails.

EXISTING BUILT PROGRAM (in operation):
- 3 independent "Glamping Suites", each one fully private with no visual or
  acoustic contact with the others. Typology: transparent inflatable BUBBLE
  DOMES (clear PVC sphere for the bedroom, with a terracotta / sand-coloured
  opaque arched annex for bathroom and entrance), placed on large ipê-wood
  decks with a private plunge pool, jacuzzi / hot tub, day-bed under a
  thatched palapa with white linen curtains, canopy bed inside, rattan
  furniture, warm string lights and lanterns at night.
  Suite 1: 200 m² private deck + 43 m² suite, pool, jacuzzi, chill-out.
  Suites 2 and 3: large deck, private pool, panoramic view of the valley.
- Reception, 50 m²: timber cabin with thatched roof, wide glass doors, natural
  wood floors, hanging rattan egg chairs, rattan tables, small coffee pantry.
- Parking for 8 cars, under trees.
- Caretaker house used for laundry, storage and maintenance.

PLANNED EXPANSION (phase 2 and 3):
- New glamping suites of different typologies (more bubble domes, safari
  tents, A-frame / cabin suites, elevated tree suites), always isolated by
  vegetation.
- Organic-shaped natural pool integrated in the landscape (lagoon style, dark
  green water, stone edges, wooden sun deck).
- Sensory trails and forest walkways.
- Fire-pit lounges under the stars.
- Outdoor wellness areas: yoga / meditation deck under a large tree, sauna,
  spa pavilion.
- Optional phase 3: a low-rise CONDO HOTEL, 40 apartments of 40 m², maximum
  2 storeys, timber and glass, set into the forest edge without cutting the
  large trees.

VISUAL STYLE (apply to EVERY image unless I say otherwise):
- Photorealistic, high-end hospitality editorial photography (Condé Nast
  Traveler / Design Hotels look). Full-frame camera, 24–35 mm lens for
  exteriors, 50 mm for interiors, shallow depth of field on details.
- Light: golden hour or blue hour; soft tropical daylight for daytime shots.
- Palette: deep greens of the rainforest, warm honey-toned wood, terracotta
  and sand, off-white linen, turquoise pool water, warm amber lights at night.
- Materials: natural wood decks, thatch, rattan, stone, linen, transparent
  domes. No plastic-looking or cartoonish rendering.
- Mood: "New Luxury" — privacy, silence, nature, quality time. Calm, intimate,
  exclusive, never flashy. Few or no people; when present, one couple, seen
  from behind or at a distance.
- Always coherent with the Brazilian Atlantic Rainforest: palms, bromeliads,
  ferns, heliconias, banana leaves, large fig trees, green hills in the
  background. No pine trees, no desert, no snow, no ocean directly on site.
- No text, no logos, no watermarks inside the image.
- Default aspect ratio 16:9 (landscape). If I ask for "vertical", use 4:5.

Confirm you understood the brief in one line and wait for my prompts.
```

## DICAS DE USO
- Gere **uma imagem por mensagem**. Se o ChatGPT devolver algo fora do estilo,
  responda: "Redo, same prompt, stricter to the VISUAL STYLE block: more
  photorealistic, warmer light, denser rainforest, no text."
- Para manter consistência entre imagens, anexe a imagem 1.2 (domo aprovado)
  às gerações seguintes e escreva: "Use the attached image as the reference
  for the dome typology, materials and lighting."
- Para o master plan, se as proporções do terreno saírem erradas, envie a
  planta ou o print do Google Earth do lote e diga: "Use the attached plot
  shape and orientation exactly."
- Os dados numéricos (3 ha, 3 suítes, deck 200 m², recepção 50 m², 8 vagas,
  40 apartamentos de 40 m², 2 pavimentos) vêm do PDF. O layout (posição das
  zonas, distâncias, contorno do terreno) é uma proposta conceitual e deve ser
  validado com planialtimetria e consulta à FLORAM antes de qualquer uso
  técnico.
